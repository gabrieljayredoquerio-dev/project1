Privacy notice

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Where your information is stored

Account details, passwords, budgets, expenses, and history are saved in this browser's local storage. They are not synchronized to a server by the app. Anyone with access to this browser profile or its developer tools may be able to view or change them.

Hosting and network requests

The app may be hosted by GitHub Pages. Loading the website sends ordinary requests to the hosting provider, which may process request information under its own privacy terms. The app itself does not send your account or budget entries to a backend.

Passwords and backups

Passwords are not protected by server-side authentication. Do not use a password that you use elsewhere. Downloaded JSON backups contain your profile name/email and financial records, but not the password; protect or delete those files carefully.

Retention and deletion

Your local data remains until you delete the account in the app or clear this site's browser storage. Removing the account only removes its data from this browser; it cannot remove downloaded backups or hosting logs.

Not for sensitive information

This app is not designed for confidential or sensitive financial information. This notice describes the app's current behavior and is not a substitute for legal advice or a formal privacy policy for a hosted service.
