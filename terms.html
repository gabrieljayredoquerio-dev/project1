AllowanceSaver is a personal budgeting tool. It is not a bank, financial adviser, or secure online account service.

Local accounts

Accounts are local browser profiles, not verified identities. Do not enter passwords used elsewhere, confidential information, or financial details that you need to keep private. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to access local data.

Data and backups

You are responsible for keeping downloaded backups safe. Browser storage can be removed by clearing site data, browser settings, or device data. The project does not promise recovery, synchronization, or uninterrupted availability.

Financial decisions

Figures and insights are for personal tracking only and may be incomplete if entries are missing or incorrect. Do not rely on the app as professional financial, tax, or legal advice.

Service limits

The app does not provide server-side accounts, synchronization, password recovery, or server backups. It is not designed for sensitive data. A hosted service with production accounts needs an appropriate backend, security controls, operational backups, and policies tailored to its audience and jurisdiction.
